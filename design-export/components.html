Reference · contract components

Components

The named contract components on the two hero screens. These are placeholders — token-styled, structurally correct — for Design to restyle by swapping the look, not the structure. They live in src/components/concierge/.

Component

ActiveTile

Right now On track

On the 16:05 to Derby

Next: Derby Station · 17:38

via train — calling at Leicester

Shows — The day-of focus: a status pill, the headline of what's happening now, the next Anchor + time, the next Leg, and (before departure) a leave-by field.

States — Urgency drives the pill colour — comfortable (sage, “On track”), urgent (amber, “Leave soon”), breach (rust, “Running late”). Leave-by shows only before you've left.

Component

AnchorCard

Appointment 09:30

Henderson & Cole

Holborn, London WC1

Shows — A fixed point on the day: a type label (Appointment / Reservation / Arrival / Flight / Check-in / Anchor), the time or window, the title, and the place.

States — Renders solid (a confirmed point). Type label varies; time may be a single time or a window; place optional. Tappable to open/edit.

Component

IntentionCard

Intention soft

With the Henderson team by 09:30

Arrive Holborn by 09:15

Leave by 06:48

Shows — A soft goal: the description, an optional target, the back-calculated leave-by, and controls to toggle it off or promote it to a hard Anchor.

States — soft (amber tag) vs promoted (green tag); active vs toggled-off (dimmed). Promote swaps the controls.

Component

GapCard

St Pancras → Henderson & Cole needs input

How are you getting from St Pancras to Henderson & Cole?

Shows — A computed hole between two Anchors with no resolved connection: the endpoints, a “needs input” tag, the binary-reveal prompt, and a Resolve action into the decision layer.

States — Types: transport / accommodation / unplanned-time / care gap. State: open · watching · resolved · dismissed. Note: the endpoint label currently uses --ink-soft (faint) — a placeholder detail for Design.

Component

LegCard

Train booked

Derby → London St Pancras

07:02 → 08:34 £48.50

1h 32m · East Midlands Railway

Shows — A resolved movement: the mode, a booking-status tag, from → to, departure → arrival, cost, and notes.

States — Booking status sets the tag — synced/booked (sage), manual (amber), not booked (rust). Cost and notes optional.

Component

ComparisonMatrix

Booking hands off to the provider for now, then absorbs the result.

Shows — The transport decision layer: the two services bracketing the target — one on-time-or-early, one acceptably-late — each with delta-vs-intention, changes and cost, then a Book action.

States — One option is selected (gold ring); the user flips between them. “Book” is the one transactional stub (§17). Empty state when there are no sensible options.
